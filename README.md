## 🖱️ Understanding Mouse DPI and Sensitivity

- **DPI** stands for "dots per inch." It measures how many pixels your cursor moves when you move your mouse one inch. Common gaming mice have DPI settings from 400 to 3200 or higher.
- **In-game sensitivity** is the multiplier applied to your mouse movement within a game. Together with DPI, it determines your effective aiming speed.

mouse-optimizer-gaming takes the guesswork out of this equation, giving you numbers you can trust.
